    How it works

    Three steps. You spend two minutes. The agents do the rest.

    1. 1

      Pick a tour and add your listing

      About 2 minutes
    2. 2

      Agents tour and rate it

      Tour morning
    3. 3

    Questions agents ask

    Who tours my listing?

    Licensed agents who signed up for that tour. Tours are organized by zip code and neighborhood, so they are the agents working your area.

    Do I need to be there?

    Your home needs to be open and show-ready at its time on the route. Many listing agents stay to meet the group, but the ratings come in either way.

    Does my seller see the report?

    Only if you share it. The report comes to you, and you decide what to pass along.

    Which areas do you cover?

    The Phoenix Valley, including Scottsdale, Cave Creek, Peoria, Glendale, Fountain Hills, Sun City West and San Tan Valley.

    What happens after I sign up?

    You get a confirmation email right away. We review the listing and email you when it is approved. Then you get reminders 48 and 24 hours before the tour.
